Nazar: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Nazar is a free app that helps you guard your gaze and your time. It pauses adult websites, X (Twitter) posts marked as adult content, YouTube Shorts, Instagram and Facebook Reels, websites you choose, and apps that pass a daily time limit you set. It is made by Khair Ullah. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

In short

Nazar has no internet access at all: it cannot send anything anywhere. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no servers. Everything it does happens on your phone.

Accessibility service

  • Nazar uses Android's Accessibility service, which you switch on yourself. It is used only to spot content you asked Nazar to pause.
  • It reads the web address bar and search boxes of browsers, which screen of YouTube, Instagram or Facebook is open, and the text visible on X (Twitter).
  • That text is checked in the phone's memory against Nazar's lists and dropped at once. It is never saved, never logged and never sent anywhere.
  • Nazar does not read your messages, passwords or other apps, and does not act for you except to go back or to the home screen and show its pause screen when something is blocked.

Usage access

  • If you set daily app limits, Nazar asks for Usage access, so it can see how long each of those apps was open today. This stays on your phone.

What is stored on your phone

  • Your settings, your blocked-site list and your app limits.
  • Counts: how many pauses of each kind happened on each day, your clean-day streak and your dhikr count. Only the kind and the day are saved, never the website, search or text that caused a pause.
  • If you set a PIN, only a scrambled (hashed) form of it is kept, not the PIN itself.

Sharing

Nazar shares no data with anyone, because it has no way to send data.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling Nazar, or clearing its storage in your phone's settings (Apps → Nazar → Storage → Clear data), deletes everything it stored.

Children

Nazar is a self-accountability tool for adults. It is not directed at children.

Changes
